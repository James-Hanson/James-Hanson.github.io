Albertus Medium — provenance note
=================================

File:     fonts/albertus-medium.ttf
Identity: "Albertus Medium", Version 1.3 (Hewlett-Packard build)
Trademark: Albertus Medium is a trademark of Monotype.
Role:     Title slide face only. All other slide text uses Latin Modern Sans.

Provenance
----------
Obtained by the author from a freely available download. Author has reviewed
the source and considers it fine to use for this deck. Decision recorded
here; no further restrictions applied in this project.

Technical note
--------------
Only one weight (Regular) is present in the source, so the title is set to
font-weight: 400. Heavier weights would trigger the browser's synthetic
(faux) bold, which distorts Albertus' flared serifs.
